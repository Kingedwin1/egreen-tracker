What you get

Everything about your money, in one calm place.

Knows who paid whom

A payment someone sends you counts as money in, not spending. Moving money between your own accounts isn’t counted as spending either.

Just tell Egom what happened

Type “I paid Emeka 20k for transport” or “got 20k from mum yesterday”. Egom works out the amount, the person and the date.

Every bank in one place

See what went through OPay, Moniepoint, Kuda, FCMB or any account. Upload a PDF statement to fill in what you forgot to send.

Budgets that speak up

Set a budget for any category. When you go over, Egom shows it clearly, so nothing creeps up on you.

Learns your categories

Fix a category once and Egom remembers that person or shop next time. Pick your kind of business and it starts with the right list.

On your phone, like an app

Add Egom to your home screen and get notifications, without downloading anything from an app store.

Privacy

We never ask for your bank login.

Egom only sees what you choose to send it. It can’t move your money, and it doesn’t need to. Read the privacy policy.

  • No bank passwords or card details, ever.
  • Only the receipts and statements you choose to send.
  • Download a copy of everything, or delete your account, any time.
  • We never sell your information or show you ads.

Questions

Good to know.

Does Egom need access to my bank account?

No. You forward your receipts or type what you spent. Egom never asks for your bank login, PIN or card details, and it can’t move your money.

Which Nigerian banks does Egom work with?

Any bank or app that gives you a transfer receipt: OPay, Moniepoint, Kuda, PalmPay, GTBank, FCMB and others. You can also upload a PDF bank statement in the app.

Can I use Egom for personal and business money?

Yes. You get a Personal side and a Business side with invoices, quotes, customers and reports, and you switch between them with one tap.

What if Egom puts something in the wrong category?

Reply “cat” and the right category on WhatsApp, or change it in the app. Egom remembers that person or shop next time.

Can I delete my records?

Yes. You can download a copy of everything, or delete your account and all your records, from the app at any time.
