Privacy policy

Effective 23 November 2026

Egom helps you keep track of money in and money out, for yourself and for your business. To do that it has to hold some of your financial information. This page explains, in plain words, what we collect, why, who handles it, who can see it, and what you can ask us to do with it.

The short version

  • Your records are yours. Other Egom users can't see them.
  • We don't sell your information and we don't show you ads.
  • A few trusted services help us run Egom (hosting, WhatsApp, reading receipts, email). They only get what they need to do their job.
  • You can ask for a copy of your information, ask us to correct it, or delete your account at any time. You can do the first and last yourself in Egom under Your account → Your data, or write to support@egom.ng.

1. Who we are

Egom (egom.ng) is a money assistant made up of a website and a WhatsApp assistant. Egom is operated by Egreen Innovative Concept Ltd, a company registered in Nigeria. In this policy, "Egom", "we" and "us" mean Egreen Innovative Concept Ltd as the operator of Egom, and "you" means anyone who uses it. For the purposes of the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, we are responsible for the information described here, except where section 6 says otherwise.

You can reach us at support@egom.ng.

2. What we collect

Information you give us

  • Your account: your email address, your name, and your password if you set one. Passwords are stored in a scrambled form that we can't read. If you turn on two-step verification, we keep the setup for your authenticator app, not your codes.
  • Your profile: your business name and the kind of business, if you add a business, and the WhatsApp number you link.
  • Your money records: amounts, dates, who you paid or who paid you, the bank or wallet used, transaction references, narrations, categories, notes, budgets and account balances you enter.
  • Your business records, if you use the Business side: your logo, brand colour, address, phone, email and bank details shown on invoices; your customers, items and prices; invoices, quotes and payments; vendors, purchases and payments to vendors; and the accounting entries Egom builds from them.
  • Messages to the Egom WhatsApp assistant: the text you send, and receipts, screenshots, bank alerts or PDFs you forward.
  • Messages to support: anything you write to us by email.

Information collected as you use Egom

  • Notification details: if you turn on notifications, your browser gives us an address to send them to.
  • Basic technical details: like any website, the services that host Egom record things such as your IP address, browser type and the time of a visit, to keep the service working and secure.
  • Settings on your device: choices like light or dark mode, and which page you last opened, are saved in your own browser, not sent to us.

We don't ask for your bank login details, card numbers or BVN, and Egom never connects to your bank account. You add records yourself or forward them to the WhatsApp assistant.

5. Who can see your information

  • You. Only you can see your Personal records. Database rules check who is signed in on every request, so one user can't read another user's records.
  • Your business. Business records are visible to the business owner. If we add staff accounts in the future, a business owner will choose who to invite and what they can do, and we'll update this policy.
  • Customers you send a link to. When you share an invoice or quote link, anyone with that link can see that one document: your business details on it, the customer's name and the items. They can't see anything else. Drafts and voided invoices can't be opened with a link.
  • The Egom team. The people who run Egom can technically access the database. We only do so when needed to run, fix or secure the service, to answer a request from you, or when the law requires it.

6. Your customers' and vendors' details

When you use the Business side, you may add details about other people, such as your customers' and vendors' names, phone numbers, emails and addresses. For those details, you (the business) decide what to collect and why, and we process them on your behalf to provide Egom. Please only add details you're allowed to use, and tell your customers how you use them where the law requires it.

7. How long we keep it

  • Your account and records: for as long as your account is open, so your history and reports stay complete.
  • Forwarded receipts and screenshots: Egom keeps the details it reads (amount, date, who, reference), not the image or PDF itself. WhatsApp keeps its own copy of the chat on your phone and its servers under Meta's terms.
  • Deleted records: removed from your account straight away. Business accounting entries are reversed rather than erased, so the books stay balanced.
  • Closed accounts: when you delete your account in Egom, your information is removed straight away. If you ask us by email, we do it within 30 days. Copies in backups are overwritten in the normal backup cycle. We may keep a limited record where the law requires it.

8. How we protect it

  • All connections to Egom are encrypted (HTTPS).
  • Database rules limit every read and write to the signed-in owner of the information.
  • You can sign in with a one-time email code or a password, and add two-step verification with an authenticator app. When two-step is on, your records stay locked until the code is entered.
  • Secret keys for the services in section 4 are stored on the server, never in the website's code.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your information, we'll tell you and the Nigeria Data Protection Commission as the law requires.

9. Your rights

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 you can:

  • ask what information we hold about you and get a copy of it;
  • ask us to correct information that's wrong or incomplete;
  • ask us to delete your information and close your account;
  • ask for your records in a common format you can take elsewhere;
  • object to, or ask us to limit, how we use your information;
  • withdraw consent you gave, such as for notifications, at any time;
  • complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (ndpc.gov.ng).

To use any of these rights, email support@egom.ng from the address on your account. We'll reply within 30 days. You can already correct or delete records, download a copy of everything, and delete your account yourself inside Egom.

10. Information stored outside Nigeria

Some of the services in section 4 store or process information on servers outside Nigeria. We use services that protect information with appropriate safeguards, as the Nigeria Data Protection Act requires for transfers abroad.

11. Children

Egom is for people aged 18 and over. We don't knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has created an account, please contact us and we'll delete it.

12. Changes and contact

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we'll update the date at the top and let you know in Egom or by email before the change takes effect.

Questions, requests or complaints: support@egom.ng
